Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Chupy is provided by Bartłomiej Bocian. For privacy questions or requests, contact old.stoork@gmail.com.

Your game progress and preferences are stored locally. Chupy has no player accounts or online leaderboard. When advertising features are available, Google processes information to provide ads and manage privacy choices, as described below.

Game progress and preferences

Chupy saves completed and unlocked levels, best scores, hearts and their refill time, and your symbol-display and haptic preferences on your device. These saves provide game functionality, including the local Ranking screen.

The app does not upload these saves to the developer or provide its own cloud synchronization. Chupy does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service beyond the advertising SDK described below.

Optional rewarded advertising

When enabled, Chupy uses Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads that refill hearts. Watching an ad is optional. You can use available hearts, wait for hearts to refill, or play Practice without watching an ad.

When advertising is available, the app checks consent information and may preload an ad on the menu or a level-result screen before you choose to watch one. Ad loading depends on the privacy choices and eligibility reported by Google’s consent service.

According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK may process:

  • IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them;
  • device and app identifiers;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • information about ads displayed and interactions with the app or ads.

Google uses this information for advertising delivery and measurement, service improvement, and fraud prevention. Actual processing depends on the device, consent choices, and ad configuration. See how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s Privacy Policy, including its information about sharing with other parties.

Chupy requests non-personalized ads, disables Google’s publisher first-party ID, and does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. These choices do not prevent all advertising-related data processing. Non-personalized ads can still use local storage or identifiers for functions such as frequency control, reporting, and fraud prevention.

Consent and privacy choices

Chupy uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent information. Privacy messages and choices depend on your region and the applicable requirements. The service stores privacy preferences and communicates with Google to update consent information.

When a consent message is shown, use its controls to make your choices. You can revisit them through Settings → Privacy options when that option is available. If you previously gave consent, you can withdraw it through those controls. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.

Ad availability may change after you update your choices. Refusing or withdrawing consent does not necessarily prevent all network activity; Google may allow limited advertising under the applicable settings. See Google’s explanation of consent and ad serving.

Storage, deletion, and backups

Local game saves do not expire automatically. To remove Chupy’s local app data, open iOS Settings, choose General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), select Chupy → Delete App. Offloading the app retains its data. The developer cannot remotely access or delete your local saves.

Device backups may include game data, depending on your system settings. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete existing backups or information held by Google. Manage backups through your device settings and backup provider.

Google retains information for periods that depend on the data and purpose, as explained in its data-retention policy. Google may process information on servers in different countries; its Privacy Policy explains international processing and safeguards.

Support correspondence

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you voluntarily include are used to answer your request and handle related follow-up. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Support messages are stored in the developer’s Gmail inbox while handling your request and any necessary follow-up. To request deletion, email old.stoork@gmail.com. Applicable retention obligations may affect deletion. Google provides Gmail under its own Privacy Policy.

Privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information, object to certain uses, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact old.stoork@gmail.com about information held by the developer.

For information processed by Google, its Privacy Policy explains the available controls and request routes. The developer cannot identify your local game saves remotely. Advertising consent choices can be changed as described above.

Children’s privacy

Chupy does not ask players to provide a name, date of birth, or contact details in the game. Advertising services may still process technical information as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Chupy or a support request, contact old.stoork@gmail.com.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The developer has added no advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, or forms to this page. Opening it does not attach your game saves to the web request.

Apple services

Apple may separately process device diagnostics and backups according to your sharing and backup settings. These services are governed by Apple’s privacy information.

Policy updates

This policy may change when Chupy or its data practices change. The current version and its update date will remain available on this page.
