Help & Support

CardVault stores your loyalty and membership cards on your device. It is developed by Bartłomiej Bocian and is free to use.

For help, questions, or feedback, email

old.stoork@gmail.com

Adding a card

Open Cards and tap Add card, or open the Scan tab. Allow camera access when asked, keep the barcode visible, avoid glare, and hold your device steady. Check the scanned number before saving.

If the camera is unavailable or a barcode is difficult to read, choose Enter number manually. Manual entry works without camera permission.

Camera permission

If you previously declined permission, open your device’s Settings, find CardVault, and enable Camera access. You can continue using manual entry if you prefer.

Trouble opening your wallet

Tap Try Again on the recovery screen. If the problem continues, close and reopen CardVault, then contact support. Retry opens the same local wallet; it does not intentionally reset your cards.

Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a troubleshooting step, because this can remove your locally stored cards. Support cannot access your wallet remotely or guarantee recovery from a damaged database.

What to include in a support message

  • Your CardVault version and build, shown in Settings and on the recovery screen.
  • Your iOS version and device model.
  • A description of what happened and the steps that led to it.

Do not send membership numbers, barcode images, personal notes, or wallet database files.

Deleting cards and managing backups

You can delete individual cards from their details or clear all cards in Settings. CardVault has no account or app-operated cloud synchronization. Device backups depend on your system settings and backup provider.
